# European-Sales-Dashboard- European Retail Sales Analysis & Interactive Excel Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes **2,700+ retail transactions** across European markets to evaluate sales performance, product category profitability, and customer distribution channels. Using Microsoft Excel, raw transactional data was structured and analyzed to deliver actionable business insights for sales optimization.

---

## Key Highlights & Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Revenue:** $645,223.18
* **Total Gross Profit:** $296,384.25
* **Gross Margin:** 45.94%
* **Total Units Sold:** 6,698 units
* **Total Transaction Volume:** 2,707 orders

---

##  Key Data Insights

### 1. Top Product Categories by Revenue
* **Camping** generated the highest overall revenue at **$198,505.13** (30.8% of total revenue) with 1,270 units sold.
* **Hiking** ranked second in revenue (**$179,959.14**) and led in overall volume with **1,598 units sold**.
* **Travel** brought in **$123,724.49** across 1,405 units.

### 2. Regional Performance Breakdown
* **Western Europe** led total sales with **$198,040.42** in revenue and **€91,749.28** in gross profit.
* **Central Europe** closely followed with **$191,158.66** in revenue.
* **Northern Europe** (€131,488.62) and **Southern Europe** ($124,535.49) accounted for the remaining regional sales.

### 3. Sales Channel Performance
* **Online Store** was the primary revenue driver, contributing **$260,307.06** (40.3% of total revenue).
* **Corporate B2B** channels generated **$179,633.50**.
* **Retail Partners** and **Marketplace** channels accounted for €112,446.61 and €92,836.01 respectively.

---

##  Tools & Technical Skills Applied
* **Data Cleaning & Formatting:** Date parsing, categorizing order types, and currency alignment.
* **Excel Formulas:** Calculated metrics including `Revenue`, `COGS`, `Gross Profit`, and `Gross Margin %`.
* **Pivot Tables & Analytics:** Categorical grouping, profit margin calculations, and multi-dimensional analysis across sales channels and European regions.
* **Interactive Dashboarding:** Built dynamic pivot charts and interactive slicers for regional and temporal filtering.
